## Group 15 - one conserved utility ledger

`UtilityPowerAccount` now explicitly distinguishes Electricity from Heat. Its shared utility owner allocates Producer/Consumer/Link typed references; a feature cannot create another energy registry or reward account. [Pure utility functions](../../../../../../../../src/tests/fixtures/cleanup/source-epochs/openttd3-75ea75718f/5fbd053f23292efb-multilayer_utility.h) operate on actual owner-supplied snapshots and return proposals only. Generation is watts; heat/electricity, demand, granted use and unmet demand are joules per saved interval. Generation integrates `watts * synchronized_ticks * 27` millijoules; the saved remainder is 0..999. The 27-ms simulation constant is compile-checked, never read from frame rate, fast-forward speed, wall time, selected layer or zoom. Conversion retains a separate 0..99 fractional-percent remainder; construction work's Q16 remainder belongs to its own job, never this meter.

Bounds: rate at most 1,000,000,000 W, duration at most 1,000,000 ticks, one generated/demand/request quantity at most 27,000,000,000,000 J. Producer, consumer and link lists each have at most 4,096 records; identity all-ones is invalid. Products and whole-pass energy totals fit `uint64_t`, verified by compile-time bounds. Duplicate IDs/eligible references, missing sources, unknown layers, zero/terminal intervals or invalid thermal roles refuse; no resource or timer changes. The live commit owner also rejects intervals not newer than its saved committed interval. A query cannot grant the same energy twice merely because a GUI asks for a new proposal.

The electrical pass snapshots generation/demand/network eligibility. It validates **all** references before planning any debit, sorts commissioned exports by stable LinkID, and reserves only the source's own generation for those exports. It credits the existing destination-layer output account exactly once. Imported energy cannot be re-exported in that interval, so a link cycle cannot circulate fresh credits repeatedly. Local consumers then sort by stable ConsumerID, with eligible sources sorted by ProducerID. A source must share the consumer's layer and be explicitly linked; coordinate overlap, catchment and display selection provide no eligibility. Source snapshots may be zero for a receiving riser account. A no-link consumer gets zero power even beside a generator.

Allocation priority is explicit exports, then local consumer ID; later UI policy changes require a versioned deterministic contract rather than container iteration order. Every grant debits the available source balance immediately in the proposal. Each source conserves `generated + imported = exported + consumed + unused`; globally exports cancel and `generation = consumption + unused`. Unused energy is curtailed at interval end unless a separately bounded saved storage facility owns it. Link requests are capped by the feature owner's commissioned capacity before planning; electrical linkage/ownership and geometric connectivity are not inferred by this numerical helper.

Commit is one simulation-thread transaction: revalidate the same interval, source/consumer/link references and network snapshot, publish each actual flow/grant/remainder once, and only then let recipes/tubes/construction consume their own grant. If anything invalidates the snapshot, cancel the proposal and retain unconsumed inventory; a retry cannot debit a previous interval again. Demand/granted/unmet are distinct status fields, not additive generation. Snapshot indexes and rendering/network-connectivity caches are rebuildable; saved interval/remainder/real material reservations are authoritative.

Heat is allocated first within one validated Deep vent/network. Direct recipes and generators request shares from the **same** bounded thermal budget, in stable ConsumerID order. Generators convert only their granted heat share at their declared efficiency; direct recipes consume a different grant. No heat export exists through an electrical riser, and electricity never substitutes for an ungranted thermal share. Solar source availability/mirror calculation happens once before publishing its generated electrical snapshot; the allocation pass never applies another mirror bonus. Geothermal and other non-solar sources are not eligible.

Connected thermal interval clarification,2026-10-09: every published collector in the common native thermal allocation phase uses the same synchronized interval duration. Each installed path cell integrates its frozen rate over that duration once for aggregate source use plus unconsumed reservations. Mixed durations refuse unchanged; unpublished sources contribute zero. Residual routing follows stable consumer/source order and deterministic installed cardinal alternatives around saturated cells; no pipe stores fractional joules, new energy or an extra fair cursor.393 physical route transport records only references; native source/slice/consumed/released fields remain the sole quantity authority.
