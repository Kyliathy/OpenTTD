## Group 1 - measured storage decision

Cavity, active excavation and solid terrain survive Clear/Rail/Industry/Station conversion and demolition. Deposit identity, reserve, survey information and later vent/job authority also survive independently. `MakeClear` resets all generic m-fields, and `DoClearSquare` calls it on demolition, so those fields cannot own excavation state. Independent two-bit storage is selected: Solid=0, Excavating=1, Excavated=2; encoding 3 is reserved/corrupt. World-tile access uses `MultiLayerMap` projection and rejects Surface, Space, void tiles and stale geometry. Lifecycle/persistence follow Groups 3/4.

The prototype uses the actual fixed sparse deposit layout: typed ID, layer, CargoLabel, hardness, company claim, finite initial/remaining reserve, visibility and bounded spans. Shape/claim behavior remains Group 2. Lookup entries in this measurement are a payload baseline; later spatial bucket/node overhead is separate.

## Group 2 - finite seam and claim authority

`GeologyDepositStore` owns at most 4,096 canonical sparse records, each with at most 64 spans of at most 64 cells. IDs are 32-bit typed values with all-ones invalid, increasing monotonically even after editor deletion. Cargo meanings/layers are checked against the frozen mine catalogue. Remaining reserve may be zero but never exceed its positive initial reserve. Same-row overlap, malformed coordinates, void cells and unsupported hardness/resource/company values refuse before insertion. Different deposits cannot intersect, including different cargo meanings; equivalent coordinates in different layers are independent.

Lookup uses 16x16 layer-local buckets with at most 32 candidate IDs and at most 64 spans per candidate. Candidate IDs are canonical, and overlaps are rejected rather than decided by iteration order. Buckets and spare vector capacity are caches; only records/spans, quantities, visibility and the monotonic next-ID cursor are saved authority. Cache rebuilding preserves all quantities and query results. Logical cache payload is reported separately from allocator capacity/node bookkeeping; Group 18 measures the completed layout.

One real company claims a deposit. The same company's disjoint future mine/job footprints may share its seam; rivals refuse. Cell reservations remain Group 10 work, not a second ore pool. A consumer debits this one reserve only when its complete transaction admits the material. Excavation through ore will report destroyed recoverable ore as an explicit reserve debit alongside Earth/Rock; that ore cannot later be mined or receive hidden freight income. Initial reserve is an exact positive multiple of the canonical shape cell count. Each newly destroyed seam cell debits `min(remaining, initial / shape_cells)` tonnes; aggregate multiple-cell debits are capped at remaining reserve. Completed cavity state prevents a repeat cut from spending the same cell quantum. Mine removal releases ownership only after its last bound producer/reservation is resolved; it preserves the geological record and consumed balance. Editor/generation deletion never reuses the ID. Depleted reserves cannot be reclaimed into new ore.

Focused `[geology-deposits],[geology-claims],[geology-index]` passed **206 assertions in three cases**. Fixtures cover intersecting/invalid shapes and IDs, unsupported resource-layer combinations, competing claims, last-unit depletion, release, editor deletion, stable fresh IDs, bounded query candidates and canonical authority replay/cache rebuilding. [Retained results](../../../zz-reach2/upgrades/2026-10/r2mlvs2-c-deposit-results.txt). Actual save transport remains Group 4; this fixture does not claim a file round trip or runtime mine/job binding.

## Group 3 - deterministic guaranteed generation

Generator version 1 derives its independent 64-bit root from the synchronized world seed through the `GEOLOGY1` domain and a specified unsigned SplitMix64 stream. Metadata remains the one mutable root owner. Geological state records the generator version; Group 4 persists the complete feature descriptors. Local generation never calls `_random`, `_interactive_random` or rendering time. Ten guaranteed seams are placed first in fixed frozen-resource order: five per rock layer. Each has 32 connected cells, 32,000 t reserve and a reserved 16x16 machinery/loading/turn envelope. Minimum supported planes are 64x64; tiny/missing-plane candidates refuse before publishing geology. Full envelope cells must lie inside the current void fence. A separate Deep envelope at (44,28)..(59,43) is reserved for Group 6's vent without pre-excavating it.

Optional seams follow mandatory minerals/vent space, capped at 32 per layer with eight local-random attempts and at most 256 stable row-major fallback regions per candidate. Failed optional placement skips that optional seam; guaranteed resources never disappear. Prepared state is published only after all mandatory checks succeed. `InitializeGame` clears old geology, and real new-world generation publishes it before company/engine/script startup. Classic allocates no rock authority. Missing legacy feature chunks will retain old cavity behavior rather than regenerate solid geology; Group 4 owns that migration.
