## Delivered behavior

Actual paid Heat-cell retirement resolves original saved paths through a borrowed reference span. The shared utility owner releases only each affected current source slice's unused quantity. Source identity, allocated/consumed history and exact original path references remain intact. The same nonallocating hook runs during actual company removal; successive retired cells cannot release a slice twice. Complete company takeover moves original source, consumer and cell control together and retains usable owned rights. Coexisting electrical-cell loss does not revoke Heat shares.

Pure removal estimates and refused occupied/invalid actions preserve cash, identities and quantities. Committed retirement invalidates a previously prepared whole-use proposal. Rebuilding charges a new installation and obtains a fresh LinkID; it cannot revive old grants. A later native interval allocates over the repaired path, preserving lifetime use and original-source conservation. No free alternate source or coordinate-based heat transfer is introduced.

Derived connected components rebuild only after committed geometry/control changes. Stable company/domain/world-tile traversal visits installed records, never rock-plane tiles. Disconnected endpoints refuse without a path search. Unchanged canonical paths are cached within bounded account/reference storage; transient residual exclusions are evaluated independently and cannot contaminate later canonical paths. Cache build/search counters are diagnostics only and never affect synchronized decisions or saved authority.

The existing393 physical wire format remains sufficient. Healthy current save/load retains exact shared and physical bytes. A valid older393 lost-cell state could retain an unused promise: `RevalidateUtilityHeatRoutes` runs after accepted physical references and expires only that unused quantity through the shared owner. It preserves consumed history and original references, advances the native mutation sequence only when needed, and is idempotent. Copied preview remains independent of live quantities and accepts retained consumed paths over retired cells while rejecting malformed references.
