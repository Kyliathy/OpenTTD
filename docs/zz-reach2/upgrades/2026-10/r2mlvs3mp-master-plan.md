## What unlocks these plans

There is no single later plan that unlocks both. The parent's [M1 excavation milestone](r2mlvs-multilayer-vehicles-and-structures.md#delivery-milestones) needs Plans 2..5 plus feature persistence and final M1 art. Plan 3 already supplies most portal infrastructure. Plan 4 supplies the controller and actual boring train that finish Plan 3's commissioning/movement cases. Plan 5 supplies the source-side exchange, secondary hauler, minimal Surface receivers and empty return that finish their recurring loop. Plan13 retains completed graphics; Plan14 continues remaining asset/runtime delivery. Plans15/16 audit save, lifecycle, multiplayer, economics and acceptance.

The immediate wall is a missing **solid-starter behavior contract across Plans 2/3/4**, not an instruction to finish an unrelated feature first. The current geology planner/pass/reservation/cut path expects an open starter footprint, while a native portal preserves solid destination ground. The focused characterization (`r2mlvs4-k-final-results.txt`; historical file unavailable) proves the refusal; it does not prove commissioning. Extend the canonical producer with an explicit portal-issued starter/support contract, then supply actual train/support/wagon participants. A free starter room would violate AD-3/AD-5.

There are also scheduling cycles inside the existing plans: Plan 4 Group 2 needs its own later saved controller, cut participants and recovery; Plan 3's final entry/exchange checks need Plans 4/5; Plan 4's haulage acceptance needs Plan 5; art integration needs live consumers; full Plan 3 Group 12 requires real tube/riser consumers. This master breaks those cycles by completing foundations and individual producer behaviors before activation and returning for integration. It never counts a declaration, placeholder or isolated participant fixture as a live loop.
